# Lively creative artwork

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. The source is retained in
`source/conversation-art.png`; exact placement exports are produced by
`Tools/store-assets/render.swift`.

## Prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: premium App Store product page header artwork for Lively, a live conversation translator. Create a refined, tactile 3D editorial illustration about two people understanding one another: two flowing sculptural speech bubbles, one vivid periwinkle violet and one warm coral, interlocking gently across a small ivory sphere, with slender sound-wave ribbons connecting them. Soft ceramic and frosted glass materials, beautiful ambient shadows, elegant contemporary design, not childish. Pale warm ivory background with subtle lavender atmosphere at edges. Very wide 21:9 composition. Keep ALL important subject shapes inside the central 43 percent of canvas width and central 40 percent of height; surrounding space is restrained atmospheric background. Key shapes centered at x 50%, y 50%. Tiny complementary spheres at far edges are optional. No text, no letters, no logo, no app icon, no device frame, no user interface, no watermark, no badges. Full-bleed opaque artwork, high resolution, ideally 3840x1646 or closest wide landscape size.

## Design references

- Flighty: benefit-led storytelling, a mix of artwork and framed app details.
- Chrome: short, legible headlines with a consistent visual system.
- United: feature callouts and app screens composed into brand graphics.

These informed the layout approach; no competitor assets were reused.
